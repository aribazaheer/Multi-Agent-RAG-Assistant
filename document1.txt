Agentic AI

Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can plan,
make decisions, use tools, and perform multiple steps to achieve a goal.

Unlike a traditional chatbot that mainly responds to a single prompt,
an agentic system can break a complex task into smaller steps and
execute those steps.

Common components of an agentic AI system include an AI model,
tools, memory, planning, and feedback mechanisms.

Agentic AI can be used for research, automation, customer support,
software development, data analysis, and document processing.
